
# 📊 E-commerce Sales Analysis (SQL Project)

## 🔹 Objective

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify top-performing products, cities, and revenue patterns.

## 🔹 Tools Used

* SQL

## 🔹 Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

## 🔹 Key Analysis Performed

* Top 10 products by sales
* Sales by category
* Category contribution percentage
* Top 10 cities by revenue
* Product performance by city

## 🔹 Key Insights

* New York, Los Angeles, and Seattle generate the highest sales.
* Technology category contributes the most revenue.
* Certain products dominate sales in specific cities.

## 🔹 Conclusion

The company should focus marketing, inventory, and logistics in high-performing cities and invest more in high-revenue product categories.
